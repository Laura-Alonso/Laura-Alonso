- **Analytics engineering** — dbt models and macros, incremental materialisations, deduplication, datamarts in BigQuery
- **Data quality & observability** — dbt tests, Elementary Data, SQLFluff, schema-drift detection, CI with GitHub Actions
- **Analysis & KPIs** — KPI definitions, dashboards and automated reporting for business stakeholders
- **Statistics & ML** — forecasting, clustering, NLP and recommender systems in Python and R

## 📈 Selected impact

- **Unified data layer:** designed a dbt pipeline in BigQuery that merges two order management systems into one analyst-ready layer, used by analysts and engineers across the organisation.
- **Order cancellation tracking:** modelled a near real-time pipeline that became the single source of truth for cancellations, preventing incorrect shipments and the associated losses.
- **Platform built from scratch:** designed the complete BigQuery architecture of a fintech loan platform — ingestion, ELT with deduplication and validation, and production datamarts.
- **Cost & performance:** moved high-frequency control events (every 5 min) to their own table, cutting query costs and speeding up processing.
